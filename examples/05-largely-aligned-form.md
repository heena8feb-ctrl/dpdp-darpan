The linked notice itemises the data, purposes, processors (CRM, email tool, India and EU
hosting), retention (demo leads deleted 12 months after last contact), rights, withdrawal
(unsubscribe link and settings), and how to complain to the Board.

## Expected review (abridged)
**Verdict: 🟢 Largely aligned (on what's visible)**

- Aligned: s.7(a) basis for demo scheduling; separate unticked newsletter consent; standalone
  notice; language option; contact person; grievance period ≤ 90 days; retention trigger.
- **Low — NOT-13:** say which data goes to EU hosting and why. That's best practice; no country
  is currently restricted under s.16.
- **Low — CON-10:** confirm newsletter consents are logged with timestamp and notice version
  (s.6(10)).
- **Open questions:** do unsubscribes propagate to the CRM within a reasonable time? Does the
  CRM vendor contract bar it from using your data for its own purposes?
