# Clause library — bad → better

| # | ❌ Avoid | Why | ✅ Use instead |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "By continuing to use this site, you agree to our Privacy Policy." | Not a clear affirmative action; no itemised notice (s.6(1), Rule 3) | Standalone notice + separate unticked box per optional purpose |
| 2 | "I agree to the Terms, Privacy Policy and to receive marketing communications." | Bundled; marketing not separate or optional (s.6(1) specific, unconditional) | "☐ I accept the Terms" (if needed) + "☐ Send me offers by email" (optional) |
| 3 | "We may use your data for business purposes including but not limited to…" | Not specific or unambiguous | List each purpose: "to deliver your order", "to send OTPs" |
| 4 | "We share your data with trusted partners and affiliates." | Recipients undescribed | "We share your address with our delivery partner [name/category] only to deliver your order." |
| 5 | "We retain your data as long as necessary." | No erasure trigger (s.8(7)) | "We delete X [period] after [trigger]; invoices kept 8 years for tax law." |
| 6 | "Your consent is irrevocable." | Withdrawal right can't be waived (s.6(2), 6(4)) | "You can withdraw consent at any time at [link]." |
| 7 | "To withdraw, write to our registered office." | Not comparable ease (s.6(4)) | Toggle in Settings + link in every email + reply STOP |
| 8 | "You agree not to raise any complaint with any authority." | Invalid (s.6(2) illustration) | Remove; add Board complaint line |
| 9 | "This call may be recorded for quality and training purposes." | Vague; "training" could mean AI training; no retention | "This call is recorded to handle your request and resolve disputes, and kept for 90 days." (+ separate ask if used for AI training) |
| 10 | "Chats may be used to improve our services." | Hidden secondary use (model training) | "☐ Let [Company] use my chats (with personal details removed) to improve the assistant." |
| 11 | "Users must be 13 or older." | Child = under 18 in India (s.2) | Age gate at 18 + verifiable parental consent route (Rule 10) |
| 12 | "We process data based on our legitimate interests." | No such ground in DPDP | Name the s.7 clause or ask for consent |
| 13 | "Untick this box if you do not wish to not receive offers." | Trick question (CCPA) and ambiguous | "☐ Send me offers" |
| 14 | "Allow access to contacts to continue." (lending/shopping app) | Unnecessary data, forced action (s.6(1) illustration; RBI digital lending) | Remove; or ask in context only for a contact-based feature, optional |
| 15 | "Data controller" / "supervisory authority" | GDPR terms — confuses users | "Data Fiduciary" isn't needed in user text — say "[Company]"; "Data Protection Board of India" |
| 16 | "Please share your Aadhaar number." | Aadhaar Act limits; over-collection | "Upload masked Aadhaar" or "Verify via DigiLocker" — only if legally required |
| 17 | "No thanks, I don't want great deals." | Confirm shaming (CCPA) | "No thanks" |
| 18 | "Accept all" (big button) + "Settings" (grey link) | Interface interference | "Accept all" · "Reject all" · "Choose" — equal weight |
