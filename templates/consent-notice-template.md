# Notice, consent and withdrawal templates

Adapt to the product. Keep the notice short at the point of collection and link to the full
policy for detail. Every bracket must be filled with specifics — never leave "[purposes]" vague.

## A. Standalone notice (Rule 3) — point of collection

> **How [Company] uses your information**
>
> **What we collect and why**
> - **Name, mobile number** — to create your account and send you OTPs to log in.
> - **Delivery address** — to deliver your order.
> - **Product serial number, purchase date** — to register your warranty and book service visits.
> - *(Only if you agree below)* **Email address** — to send you offers and new-product news.
>
> **Who else handles it:** our delivery partners and our cloud and SMS service providers, only to
> do the above. [Disclose if data is processed outside India.]
>
> **How long we keep it:** [plain retention summary, e.g. "until you close your account; invoices
> for 8 years because tax law requires it"].
>
> **Your choices and rights:** you can withdraw any consent at any time at **[Settings → Privacy]**
> or **[link]** — as easily as you gave it. You can also ask to see, correct or delete your
> data, nominate someone to act for you, or raise a complaint at **[link / email]**. We'll
> respond within **[≤ 90] days**.
>
> **Questions:** [Name/role, e.g. Data Protection Officer], **[email]**, **[phone]**.
>
> **Not satisfied?** You can complain to the **Data Protection Board of India** after using our
> grievance process. [Link to Board once available.]
>
> 🌐 **Read this in:** English · हिन्दी · [other languages offered]
>
> [Full privacy policy] · Version [x], [date]

## B. Consent controls

Required (service) — no checkbox needed if the data is only used for the service; the notice
covers it (s.7(a)). If you do ask for consent to the core service, keep it separate from
optional purposes.

Optional purposes — **one unticked box each**:
```
☐ Send me offers and product news by email.
☐ Send me offers on WhatsApp.
☐ Use my purchase history to personalise recommendations in the app.
☐ Share my contact details with [named partner] so they can offer me [specific thing].
```
Then a neutral button: **[Create account]** — never "Agree and continue".

## C. Short form (for space-constrained UI, e.g. a footer form)
> We'll use your mobile number only to call you back about this enquiry.
> ☐ Also send me offers on WhatsApp. You can stop anytime by replying STOP.
> [How we use your data] · [हिन्दी]

## D. Voice / IVR opening (adapt)
> "Hello, this is [Company]'s automated assistant. This call is recorded to handle your service
> request and resolve any disputes, and kept for [90] days. We'll use your name, phone number and
> product details only to book and update your service. To hear this in Hindi, press 2 [or say
> 'Hindi']. You'll also get an SMS with our privacy notice and how to contact us."
Separate ask, only if relevant:
> "Would you like us to call or message you about extended-warranty plans? Say 'yes' or 'no'."
(Record the answer, timestamp and call ID as the consent record. A "no" must not trigger a pitch.)

## E. Withdrawal screen
> **Your privacy choices**
> Email offers ● On ○ Off
> WhatsApp offers ● On ○ Off
> Personalised recommendations ● On ○ Off
> Changes take effect within [x] days. Turning these off won't affect your orders or warranty.
> [Delete my account] · [Download / see my data] · [Nominate someone] · [Raise a complaint]

## F. Parental consent (child users)
> **Your child wants to use [App]**
> Before they can, we need a parent's or lawful guardian's permission. We'll verify you're an
> adult using [DigiLocker / your existing verified [Company] account]. We'll collect only
> [itemised data] to [purpose]. We do **not** show your child targeted ads or track their
> behaviour for advertising.
> ☐ I am [child]'s parent / lawful guardian and I agree to the above.
> [Verify and agree] · [Decline]

## G. Legacy notice (s.5(2)) — existing users
> **We've updated how we explain your data**
> You gave us your information before India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act applied.
> Here's what we hold, why, and your choices: [standalone notice A]. You can withdraw consent
> or ask us to delete your data at any time at [link].
